Frequently asked questions

Everything students and clinicians ask about Aboy AI.

What is Aboy AI?

Aboy AI is a cited medical AI assistant for healthcare students, doctors, nurses, pharmacists, educators and hospital staff. It answers clinical and study questions with source citations, shows relevant medical diagrams, and adapts to your role and level.

Does Aboy AI cite its sources?

Yes. Every answer is grounded in verified medical sources such as WHO, CDC, NICE, PubMed and StatPearls, with inline citations. It will not invent references — if a source isn't available it simply answers without citing one.

Who is Aboy AI for?

Medical and nursing students, doctors, nurses, pharmacists, lab technicians, educators, researchers, and non-clinical hospital staff such as records, reception and ward operations. Each role gets tailored answers and visuals.

Is Aboy AI free?

Yes, Aboy AI is free to use.

Can Aboy AI show medical diagrams?

Yes. For visual topics like anatomy, ECGs, histology and drug structures, Aboy AI automatically shows a relevant, English-labelled medical diagram with proper licensing and attribution.

Does Aboy AI support voice and camera?

Yes. You can ask questions by voice, hold a spoken conversation, point your camera at a problem, and have answers read back to you hands-free.

Is Aboy AI a medical device?

No. Aboy AI is an educational tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prescribe and is not a substitute for professional clinical judgment, supervision or institutional protocols.
